The OdotNYC Story

OdotNYC merch is more than clothing. It is streetwear merged with technology, built by Odot himself as a way to connect design, culture, and interactivity.

Each product can become a collector item through hidden digital features like QR codes, NFC tags, AR pop-ups, unlockable videos, music, animations, behind-the-scenes stories, and limited product experiences.

The goal is simple: every piece should feel like something you wear, collect, scan, and experience.
